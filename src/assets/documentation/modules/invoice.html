Invoice Module Documentation

Module Description

The Invoice module is used to manage and track invoices related to library purchases or transactions. It includes fields for recording essential details such as the invoice number, dates, shipping costs, and status.

Fields and Descriptions

Invoice Number:

  • Description: A unique identifier assigned to the invoice for tracking and reference purposes.
  • Usage: Enter the invoice number as provided by the vendor or system.
  • Example: "INV-2024-0001"
  • Validation: Must be a unique string or number.

Shipment Date:

  • Description: The date when the items were shipped or dispatched.
  • Usage: Enter the shipment date in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
  • Example: "15/07/2024"
  • Validation: Must be a valid date format (dd/mm/yyyy).

Billing Date:

  • Description: The date when the invoice was issued or billed.
  • Usage: Enter the billing date in the format dd/mm/yyyy.
  • Example: "16/07/2024"
  • Validation: Must be a valid date format (dd/mm/yyyy).

Shipping Cost:

  • Description: The cost associated with shipping the items.
  • Usage: Enter the shipping cost amount.
  • Example: "50.00"
  • Validation: Must be a positive monetary value.

Shipping Fund:

  • Description: The fund or budget category from which the shipping cost will be covered.
  • Usage: Enter the fund or budget category for shipping expenses.
  • Example: "Library Operations Fund"
  • Validation: Must be a valid fund or budget category.

Status:

  • Description: The current status of the invoice.
  • Usage: Select the current status of the invoice from the available options.
  • Example: "Open"
  • Validation: Must be selected from predefined status options (e.g., Open, Paid, Pending).

Show Inactive:

  • Description: Indicates whether inactive or closed invoices should be displayed.
  • Usage: Select "Yes" to show inactive invoices, or "No" to hide them.
  • Example: "No"
  • Validation: Must be a boolean value (Yes or No).
