Vendor Module Documentation

Module Description

The Vendor module manages the details of vendors supplying items or services to the library. It includes fields for contact information, invoicing details, tax information, and additional notes.

Fields and Descriptions

Name:

  • Description: The name of the vendor or company.
  • Usage: Enter the full name of the vendor.
  • Example: "Books & More Ltd."
  • Validation: Must be a valid string.

Address:

  • Description: The physical address of the vendor.
  • Usage: Enter the street address, city, state, and postal code.
  • Example: "123 Book Street, Biblioville, CA 90210"
  • Validation: Must be a valid string.

Phone:

  • Description: The primary phone number for contacting the vendor.
  • Usage: Enter the phone number.
  • Example: "+1 555-123-4567"
  • Validation: Must be a valid phone number format.

Fax:

  • Description: The fax number for the vendor.
  • Usage: Enter the fax number.
  • Example: "+1 555-765-4321"
  • Validation: Must be a valid fax number format.

Website:

  • Description: The website URL of the vendor.
  • Usage: Enter the website URL.
  • Example: "http://www.booksandmore.com"
  • Validation: Must be a valid URL.

Account Number:

  • Description: The account number assigned to the vendor by the library.
  • Usage: Enter the account number.
  • Example: "ACCT123456"
  • Validation: Must be a valid account number format.

Position:

  • Description: The job position of the contact person at the vendor.
  • Usage: Enter the contact person's job title.
  • Example: "Sales Manager"
  • Validation: Must be a valid string.

Phone (Contact Person):

  • Description: The phone number of the contact person at the vendor.
  • Usage: Enter the contact person's phone number.
  • Example: "+1 555-234-5678"
  • Validation: Must be a valid phone number format.

Alternative Phone:

  • Description: An alternative phone number for contacting the vendor or contact person.
  • Usage: Enter an alternative phone number if available.
  • Example: "+1 555-345-6789"
  • Validation: Must be a valid phone number format.

Fax (Contact Person):

  • Description: The fax number of the contact person at the vendor.
  • Usage: Enter the contact person's fax number.
  • Example: "+1 555-456-7890"
  • Validation: Must be a valid fax number format.

Email:

  • Description: The email address for contacting the vendor.
  • Usage: Enter the email address.
  • Example: "contact@booksandmore.com"
  • Validation: Must be a valid email address format.

Notes:

  • Description: Additional notes or comments about the vendor.
  • Usage: Enter any relevant information or comments.
  • Example: "Preferred vendor for educational materials."
  • Validation: Must be a valid string.

Invoice Prices (Rupees):

  • Description: The prices listed on the invoice in Rupees.
  • Usage: Enter the invoice prices in Rupees.
  • Example: "1500.00"
  • Validation: Must be a positive monetary value.

Invoice Prices (Dollar):

  • Description: The prices listed on the invoice in Dollars.
  • Usage: Enter the invoice prices in Dollars.
  • Example: "20.00"
  • Validation: Must be a positive monetary value.

Invoice Prices (Euro):

  • Description: The prices listed on the invoice in Euros.
  • Usage: Enter the invoice prices in Euros.
  • Example: "18.00"
  • Validation: Must be a positive monetary value.

Tax Number Registered:

  • Description: Indicates whether the vendor is registered for tax purposes.
  • Usage: Select "Yes" if the vendor is registered, otherwise select "No".
  • Example: "Yes"
  • Validation: Must be a boolean value (Yes or No).

Invoice Prices: Include Tax:

  • Description: Indicates whether the invoice prices include tax.
  • Usage: Select "Yes" if the prices include tax, otherwise select "No".
  • Example: "No"
  • Validation: Must be a boolean value (Yes or No).

Tax Rate:

  • Description: The rate of tax applied to the invoice prices.
  • Usage: Enter the tax rate as a percentage.
  • Example: "15" (for a 15% tax rate)
  • Validation: Must be a valid percentage value.

Discount:

  • Description: Any discounts applied to the invoice prices.
  • Usage: Enter the discount amount or percentage.
  • Example: "10" (for a 10% discount)
  • Validation: Must be a positive monetary value or percentage.

Delivery Time:

  • Description: The estimated time for delivery of items from the vendor.
  • Usage: Enter the delivery time in days or weeks.
  • Example: "7 days"
  • Validation: Must be a valid time period string.

Additional Notes:

  • Description: Any additional information or special instructions related to the vendor.
  • Usage: Enter any relevant notes or instructions.
  • Example: "Preferred for large bulk orders."
  • Validation: Must be a valid string.
