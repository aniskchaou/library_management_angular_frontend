Hold Module Documentation

Module Description

The Hold module allows library staff to manage the placing of holds on items by members. It includes fields for entering the item barcode and the member's name who is placing the hold.

Fields and Descriptions

Item Barcode:

  • Description: The barcode of the item being placed on hold.
  • Usage: Scan or enter the barcode of the item.
  • Example: "Barcode 1"
  • Validation: Must be a valid barcode string.

Member Name:

  • Description: The name of the member placing the hold.
  • Usage: Enter the member's full name.
  • Example: "John Doe"
  • Validation: Must be a valid string.
