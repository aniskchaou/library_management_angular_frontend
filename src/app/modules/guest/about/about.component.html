Our Mission

We believe that access to information is a fundamental right. Our mission is to provide free, equitable access to knowledge and foster a love of learning for every member of our community — from the youngest reader to the most seasoned researcher.

We are committed to preserving and sharing the collective heritage of human knowledge through up-to-date collections, digital resources, and inclusive community programs.
